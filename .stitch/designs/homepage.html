02 — The Atelier Philosophy

Rare Botanicals, Ancient Woods & Modern Extraction

Standard Western perfumery quickly evaporates under tropical heat. At Maison Aureate, we formulate specifically for longevity in Myanmar’s climate by marrying high-density, resinous base woods from Tanintharyi and Shan State with micro-fractionated French floral extractions.

Every creation is macerated in temperature-regulated dark glass carboys for four continuous months in Bahan, Yangon, allowing the volatile molecules to intertwine naturally without artificial stabilizers or synthetic fixatives.
